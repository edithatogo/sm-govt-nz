# Archive Schedule Documentation

## Overview

This document describes the scheduled GitHub Actions workflows that automate
non-credentialed government source archiving for the NZ Government Archive project.

All workflows use public APIs and feeds — no credentials or tokens are required.

## Scheduled Workflows

### 1. Master Archive Workflow (`archive_registered_sources.yml`)
- **Schedule**: Daily at 02:00 UTC (`cron: "0 2 * * *"`)
- **Source types**: All feasible (RSS, Bluesky, website_page, YouTube)
- **Purpose**: Comprehensive daily capture of all non-credentialed sources
- **Manual trigger**: `workflow_dispatch` with source_type, agency_id, dry_run, publish, commit_payloads parameters

### 2. RSS Scheduled (`archive_rss_scheduled.yml`)
- **Schedule**: Daily at 02:07 UTC (`cron: "7 2 * * *"`)
- **Source type**: RSS
- **Purpose**: Daily RSS feed capture across all agencies with feeds
- **Manual trigger**: `workflow_dispatch` with agency_id, dry_run, commit_payloads

### 3. Bluesky Scheduled (`archive_bluesky_scheduled.yml`)
- **Schedule**: Every 6 hours (`cron: "7 */6 * * *"`)
- **Source type**: Bluesky
- **Purpose**: Frequent Bluesky post capture from 4 government accounts
- **Manual trigger**: `workflow_dispatch` with agency_id, dry_run, commit_payloads

### 4. Website Scheduled (`archive_website_scheduled.yml`)
- **Schedule**: Weekly on Sunday at 02:07 UTC (`cron: "7 2 * * 0"`)
- **Source type**: website_page
- **Purpose**: Weekly homepage archiving for 247 government agencies
- **Manual trigger**: `workflow_dispatch` with agency_id, dry_run, page_limit, commit_payloads

### 5. YouTube Scheduled (`archive_youtube_scheduled.yml`)
- **Schedule**: Weekly on Sunday at 02:17 UTC (`cron: "17 2 * * 0"`)
- **Source type**: YouTube
- **Purpose**: Weekly YouTube video metadata capture for 182 channels
- **Manual trigger**: `workflow_dispatch` with agency_id, dry_run, channel_limit

### 6. Archive Health Monitor (`archive_health_monitor.yml`)
- **Schedule**: Weekly on Monday at 03:00 UTC (`cron: "0 3 * * 1"`)
- **Purpose**: Check archive staleness and create GitHub Issues for stale sources
- **Script**: `scripts/check_archive_staleness.py`
- **TTLs**: RSS=24h, Bluesky=6h, Website=7d, YouTube=7d

### 7. Existing Sources Archive (`archive_sources.yml`)
- **Schedule**: Every 6 hours (`cron: "7 */6 * * *"`)
- **Purpose**: Captures Courts NZ Bluesky, RSS, and website sources
- **Note**: Pre-existing workflow for courts-of-nz specifically

## Archive State Tracking

- `conductor/archive_state.json` — Tracks last-capture timestamps per agency per source type
- `conductor/archive_source_health.json` — Per-source health status
- `conductor/archive_health_report.json` — Generated by health monitor
