Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of project-held data associated with the mirror app.

Last updated: 14 June 2026

This project primarily handles public-sector public posts and public mirror-account metadata. It does not intentionally collect private user data from platform users.

Request Deletion

Open an issue at github.com/edithatogo/sm-govt-nz/issues with enough detail to identify the record, account, URL, or platform identifier you want reviewed.

What Can Be Deleted

Project-maintained copies, derived metadata, and mirror records can be reviewed for deletion or correction. Public source material controlled by the original platform or public-sector account must be removed by that source before this project can treat the source record as no longer public.

Response Handling

Requests will be reviewed against the public-interest archive purpose, platform requirements, and any applicable legal obligations. If a record is removed from the project archive, future generated datasets will omit that record unless it is restored by a public source and intentionally recaptured.
